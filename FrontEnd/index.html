CAMPUS GUIDE

FREQUENTLY ASKED

ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS
Who can use Campus Mart?
Campus Mart is designed specifically for university students, faculty, and campus residents. Any student with an institutional email can register, discover items, purchase, negotiate offers, and list products for sale.
How do I list an item for sale?
Log into your student account and click "List Item" in the navigation bar. Provide product details, select a category, enter price in Indian Rupees (₹), specify condition, and upload photos. Your item goes live immediately on the catalogue.
How do price offers and auctions work?
For fixed price items, you can submit an offer directly on the product detail page, which the seller can accept or decline in their dashboard. For auction listings, you can enter competitive bids before the timer expires.
How do student pickups work safely?
Campus Mart listings include seller hostel block locations. Once an order or offer is confirmed, both parties can coordinate handoffs in designated campus zones like library lobbies, cafeteria commons, or hostel gates.
Can I edit or deactivate my listing later?
Yes. Navigate to "My Listings" from your account menu to edit prices, update condition notes, or remove items once they have been purchased by a classmate.
