## 🧭 Overview

**CampusMart** is a campus-focused marketplace designed specifically for college students.

It allows students to **buy, sell, discover, save, donate and exchange products**, while also providing access to campus essentials, academic help and student-oriented services.

Instead of depending on WhatsApp groups or scattered social media posts, CampusMart provides a centralized platform for campus commerce.

### 🎓 CampusMart = Your Campus + Your Marketplace

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students frequently need products such as:

- 📚 Books and study material
- 💻 Electronics and accessories
- 🎒 Bags and stationery
- 👕 Clothes and fashion items
- 🪑 Hostel and room essentials

At the same time, other students may already own these products and want to sell or donate them.

Currently, these transactions are often managed through:

- WhatsApp groups
- Personal contacts
- Social media
- Informal student communities

This makes product discovery difficult and transactions less organized.

**CampusMart solves this by providing a dedicated digital marketplace for students.**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objective of CampusMart is to create a **centralized, affordable and convenient marketplace for students**.

### Key Objectives

- 🛒 Simplify campus buying and selling
- 🔎 Make product discovery easier
- 💰 Encourage affordable second-hand purchases
- 🤝 Connect student buyers and sellers
- ♻️ Promote reuse of usable products
- ❤️ Enable donations
- 📚 Provide academic support
- 🏪 Provide campus essentials through the Tuck Shop

---

## 💡 Why CampusMart?

Unlike general e-commerce platforms, CampusMart is specifically designed around **student-to-student interaction**.

| Platform | Campus Focus | Student-to-Student |
|---|---:|---:|
| Amazon | ❌ | ❌ |
| OLX | ❌ | ✅ |
| WhatsApp Groups | ⚠️ | ✅ |
| **CampusMart** | **✅** | **✅** |

Our goal is not to replace large e-commerce platforms, but to solve a **campus-specific problem**.

# ✨ Key Features

## 🏠 1. Home Page

Introduces CampusMart and provides easy navigation to the marketplace and authentication pages.

## 🔐 2. Login & Sign Up

Allows users to create accounts and access the CampusMart platform.

## 📊 3. Buyer Dashboard

Centralized access to:

- Marketplace
- Wishlist
- Orders
- Tuck Shop
- Donations
- Academic Help
- Services

## 🛍️ 4. Marketplace

Students can:

- Browse products
- Search products
- Explore categories
- View product details
- Buy products
- Make offers

## ❤️ 5. Wishlist

Users can save products they are interested in and access them later.

## 📦 6. Orders

Provides users with access to their order-related information.

## 🏪 7. Tuck Shop

A digital campus convenience store for everyday student essentials such as stationery, snacks and other campus necessities.

## 🏷️ 8. Selling

Students can create listings by adding:

- Product details
- Price
- Description
- Images

## 💰 9. Offers & Bidding

Buyers can make offers on eligible products, allowing negotiation between buyers and sellers.

## ❤️ 10. Donations

Students can donate usable products such as:

- Books
- Clothes
- Stationery
- Furniture
- Electronics

This promotes reuse and reduces waste.

## 📚 11. Academic Help

Provides students with access to academic resources and peer-support opportunities.

## 🛠️ 12. Student Services

A platform for discovering and offering useful student-oriented services.
