# 🎓 CampusMart

### Student User Guide

> **Your Campus. Your Marketplace.**

CampusMart is a student-focused digital marketplace where students can **buy, sell, bid, negotiate, discover services, get academic help, donate items, and purchase campus essentials** from one platform.

This guide explains how a student can use CampusMart step-by-step.

# 🚀 1. Getting Started

Open the CampusMart website.

The **Home Page** is the first page students see.

### From the Home Page, students can:

* Browse the marketplace
* Buy products
* Sell products
* View services
* Find academic help
* Explore donations
* Visit the Tuck Shop
* Login or create an account

# 👤 2. Creating an Account

If you are a new student, click the **Sign Up** button.

### Step 1

Click **Sign Up** from the navigation bar.

### Step 2

Fill in your details:

* Full Name
* Email
* Password
* Confirm Password
* Phone Number
* College
* Account Type

### Step 3

Select whether you want to use CampusMart as a:

* **Buyer**
* **Seller**

### Step 4

Click **Create Account**.

After successful registration, you can log in to CampusMart.

# 🛍️ 5. Browsing the Marketplace

To find products, open **Marketplace**.

You will see products listed by other students.

### You can:

1. Search for a product.
2. Select a category.
3. Apply filters.
4. Sort products.
5. Open a product.
6. Add it to your Wishlist.
7. Add it to your Cart.

### Example

If you need a second-hand calculator:

**Marketplace → Search "Calculator" → Select Product → View Details**

# 🔎 6. Viewing a Product

# ❤️ 7. Adding a Product to Wishlist

Found something interesting but don't want to buy it yet?

Click the **❤️ Wishlist** button.

### To view saved products:

**Navigation Bar → Wishlist**

From there, you can:

* View saved products
* Remove products

# 🛒 8. Adding a Product to Cart

If you want to purchase a product, click:

**Add to Cart**

The product will be added to your shopping cart.

Then click the **Cart** icon in the navigation bar.

Inside the Cart you can:

* Change quantity
* Remove products
* Review selected products
* Check total amount
* Proceed to checkout

# 💳 9. Buying a Product

Once you are satisfied with your cart:

### Step 1

Open **Cart**.

### Step 2

Review the selected products.

### Step 3

Check the total price.

### Step 4

Click **Checkout / Place Order**.

### Step 5

Your order will be added to your **Orders** section.

> **Note:** The current project is a front-end prototype, so payment/order processing is simulated.

# 📦 10. Checking Orders

After purchasing something, open:

**Orders**

Here you can view your previous and current purchases.

Order information can include:

* Product
* Quantity
* Price
* Seller
* Order status
* Purchase information

After completing an eligible transaction, you may also be able to rate the seller.




# 🤝 11. Making an Offer

Some sellers allow buyers to negotiate the price.

### Step 1

Open the product.

### Step 2

Click **Make an Offer**.

### Step 3

Enter the amount you are willing to pay.

### Step 4

Submit the offer.

The seller can then review your offer.

The seller may:

* Accept the offer
* Reject the offer

# 🏷️ 12. Selling a Product

Want to sell something to another student?

Click **Sell**.

You will be taken to the **Create Listing** page.

### Step 1 — Enter Product Information

Enter:

* Product name
* Description
* Category
* Condition
* Price
* Quantity
* Pickup location

### Step 2 — Select Listing Type

Choose:

**Fixed Price**

or

**Auction / Bidding**

### Step 3 — Add Product Image

Upload an image of your product.

### Step 4 — Publish

Review your information and click:

**Publish Product Listing**

Your product will now appear in the marketplace.

# 💾 How Data Works

CampusMart is currently a **front-end prototype**.

The application uses browser `localStorage` to store application data such as:

* User information
* Login/session information
* Products
* Cart
* Wishlist
* Orders
* Bids
* Offers

Therefore, the data is stored **locally in the browser**.

### Important

If the browser's local storage is cleared, locally stored application data may be reset.

# ⚠️ Important Notes for Students

### 1. Use genuine information

When creating listings, provide accurate product information.

### 2. Verify products before purchasing

Always check:

* Product condition
* Price
* Seller information
* Pickup details

### 3. Meet safely

For physical transactions, prefer safe and public campus locations.

### 4. Don't share sensitive information

Never share:

* Passwords
* OTPs
* Banking PINs
* Card details

### 5. Academic Help

Use the Academic Help section for learning and legitimate assistance.
